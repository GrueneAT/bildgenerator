01Strategy

Strategy summary

Three bildgenerator UX additions on top of the Barlow base: a descriptively-labelled 2-option text-font picker (Vollkorn added as the accent serif, default stays Barlow), an own-logo hint in the logo step, and a font-usage examples page. No brand names in the UI labels; no vendoring.

  • Descriptive labels, not brand names.

    "Standardschrift — Headlines & Fließtext" (Barlow) / "Betonte Serifenschrift — für Zitate & Akzente" (Vollkorn).

  • Per-family preload fix.

    preloadFonts() now binds each PRELOAD_FONTS entry to its own family, so Vollkorn actually preloads (no serif fallback). Verified on rendered images.

  • Vollkorn via Google Fonts.

    weight 900 upright (matches DS import), link survives into build/.

03Decisions

Key decisions & options

UI labels describe type/use, not brand

User requirement

Users don't know "Vollkorn"/"Barlow" — both options are labelled by what they are and what they're for, with a hint linking to the examples page.

Vollkorn 900 upright, no italic

DS parity

Matches the design-system import Vollkorn:ital,wght@0,400;0,900 (upright only). Pinned in FONTS.OPTIONS.

Scoped <style> in examples page

Deviation

The hosted DS has no image component and the standalone page links no app CSS; a small scoped head block styles only figure/img (no inline styles, no invented classes).

Discovered (out of scope)

build.js copyAssets() nests build/resources/resources/ on incremental rebuilds; clean builds (CI) are correct. Noted for future cleanup.
